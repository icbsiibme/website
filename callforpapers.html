Track List

ICBSII 2027 is an opportunity for engineering professionals from around the world who are involved in the field of healthcare to submit their original research papers which have not been submitted elsewhere in other conferences / journals. Accepted papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements. Best papers will be awarded under each session.



  • Track 1 - Biomedical Signal & Image Processing

    • Sub Tracks
    • Biomedical Signal Processing
    • Biomedical Imaging Processing, Data Visualization techniques
    • Computer-Aided Diagnosis
    • Medical Image Mining and Retrieval Systems

  • Track 2 - Robotics, Rehabilitation, and Assistive Systems

    • Sub Tracks
    • Surgical and Rehabilitation Robotics
    • Assistive Technologies
    • Remote Health Monitoring Systems
    • Human-Robot Interaction in Medicine
    • XR for Medical Training and Rehabilitation, Computer Vision

  • Track 3 - Biomedical Devices and Instrumentation

    • Sub Tracks
    • Advances in Medical Device Design
    • Body Area Networks, Bio-Embedded Systems
    • Telemedicine Platforms, IOT in Healthcare

  • Track 4 - Bioengineering & Nanotechnology

    • Sub Tracks
    • Tissue Engineering and Regenerative Medicine
    • Nanotechnology in Medicine and Biotechnology
    • Biomaterials, 3D Bioprinting, and Drug Delivery Systems
    • Nano-enabled Diagnostics and Therapeutics

  • Track 5 - Sensors, Biophotonics, and Wearable Systems

    • Sub Tracks
    • Biophotonics in Clinical Diagnostics
    • Photonic Sensors for Health Monitoring
    • Wearable Sensors and Smart Textiles
    • Biosensors for Medical Diagnosis

  • Track 6 - Neural Engineering

    • Sub Tracks
    • Brain–Computer Interfaces (BCI)
    • Neuromodulation Techniques
    • Development of Tools for High-dimensional Neural Data
    • Cognitive Neuroscience Applications in Healthcare
